Figure 5. CloudraBench task-bank design. (a) The 50 source tasks are assigned to Easy (20), Normal (10), Hard (10), and Impossible (10). The annotations summarize the intended evaluation focus: elementary transformations and conformational changes, multi-step evidence continuity, competing stereochemical pathways, and the boundaries of prescribed methods and electronic-state treatments. These are predefined task-bank categories, not categories derived from the projection. (b) PCA of saved BAAI/bge-m3 embeddings of the original task names (PC1, 11.0%; PC2, 7.2% of variance). All 50 tasks are plotted; E1, NB1, HA1, and IA1 are labeled as the four tasks used for repeated evaluation. Color and marker shape encode the existing tier labels. This two-dimensional semantic projection illustrates topic coverage within this task bank; it neither establishes numerical difficulty nor measures chemical-space coverage. No Qwen-rewritten task descriptions are used.
