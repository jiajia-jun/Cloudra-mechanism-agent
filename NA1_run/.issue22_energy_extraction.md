## [Bug] `final_energy_hartree` returns the STARTING-geometry energy for a NON-converged ORCA MACE ExtOpt geometry optimization

### Summary
For an ORCA geometry optimization driven by an external MLIP (MACE via `! ExtOpt` + `%method ProgExt`), `log_parser` (and `orca_frames.final_energy_hartree`) returns the **initial (starting) geometry** energy, not the best/final optimized geometry energy, whenever the optimization **does not converge** (hits `%geom MaxIter`). The error is large (~0.126 Eh ≈ 79 kcal/mol) and silently propagates into energy/barrier/thermochemistry reports.

### Repro
- Input: `! ExtOpt Opt` on `[O].C=C[O-].CC=O` (16 atoms, charge −1), `%method ProgExt /Users/cloudrayyy/miniconda3/envs/chemai/bin/mace` + `Ext_Params "--model .../MACE-omol-0-extra-large-1024.model --device cpu --dtype float64"` (job 27, NA1).
- Log: `NA1_run/.cloudra/calc-27.out.log`.
  - line 452: `FINAL SINGLE POINT ENERGY (From external program)   -383.418935837100`  ← **STARTING geometry**
  - Per-cycle energies are printed as `Current Energy ....  -383.544742265 Eh` (last).
  - `I1_complex_mace_opt.xyz` header: `E -383.544742264700` (best / final geometry).
  - Tail: `****ORCA TERMINATED NORMALLY****` (so it also triggers issue #21: non-converged ⇒ judged done).
- `log_parser` returned `scf_energy_hartree = -383.4189358371` = **initial**, not the optimized −383.54474.

### Root cause
In `cloudra/tools/core/orca_frames.py`:
```python
_FINAL_ENERGY_RE = re.compile(rf"FINAL SINGLE POINT ENERGY(?:\s*\([^)]*\))?\s+{_FLOAT}")

def final_energy_hartree(text: str) -> float | None:
    """外部 MLIP (ExtOpt/ProgExt) 日志没有 SCF 能量段，唯一能量行是
    ``FINAL SINGLE POINT ENERGY (From external program)``；几何优化每步
    打印一次，取最后一次。"""
    energy = None
    for match in _FINAL_ENERGY_RE.finditer(text):
        energy = float(match.group(1).replace("D", "E").replace("d", "e"))
    return energy
```
- `finditer` keeps the LAST `FINAL SINGLE POINT ENERGY` match. The docstring's assumption is that ORCA "prints once per geometry step, take the last".
- **This assumption breaks when the optimization does NOT converge.** For a converged MACE opt, ORCA prints `FINAL SINGLE POINT ENERGY` twice (start + converged end) — verified on job 25, which is why job 25's energy is correct. For a non-converged (max-cycles) opt, ORCA prints it **only once at the starting geometry** and never emits a final energy line, so the last match is the initial energy.
- The per-cycle energy the regex ignores is `Current Energy ...` lines. For a non-converged opt, the best (current) geometry energy lives only in the last `Current Energy` / the final `.xyz`.

### Impact
- Any downstream consumer that trusts `log_parser.energy` / `scf_energy_hartree` for an ORCA MACE `! ExtOpt Opt` job that failed to converge gets a value ~0.126 Eh (~79 kcal/mol) too high (less bound), i.e. it reports the STARTING geometry energy as if it were the optimized minimum.
- This compounds issue #21 (non-converged judged `done`/`is_success=true`): an agent sees state=DONE and a plausible energy and would happily use the non-minimum geometry + wrong energy for a barrier/thermochemistry.

### Suggested fix
- For geometry-optimization / multi-point logs, prefer the **last `Current Energy`** line (fall back to the last `FINAL SINGLE POINT ENERGY` for single-point/SP logs), and/or require an explicit `optdone` (cclib `_opt_converged`) gate before trusting the returned energy as a stationary minimum.
- At minimum, `final_energy_hartree` should not claim to return the optimized energy when the log ends non-converged.
