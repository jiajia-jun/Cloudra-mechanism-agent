# GROMACS 2020Ԥ������� — 知识卡片

## 1. Density-guided simulations

Density-guided simulations allow fitting atoms into three-dimensional density maps, enabling the use of experimental density distributions as restraints during simulation.

---

## 2. gmxapi

Inclusion of gmxapi 0.1 provides an API and user interface for managing complex simulations, data flow, and pluggable molecular dynamics extension code, enhancing the reliability of the simulation workflow.

---

## 3. New modular simulator

A new modular simulator can be built from individual objects describing different calculations happening at each simulation step.

---

## 4. Parrinello-Rahman pressure coupling for md-vv

Parrinello-Rahman pressure coupling is now available for the md-vv integrator.

---

## 5. Tool changes

Tools such as convert-trj and extract-cluster were removed in the update.

---

## 6. Removal of group cut-off scheme

The group cut-off scheme has been removed, causing several kinds of simulations that depend on it to no longer work.

---

## 7. Vacuum simulations

Simulations under vacuum conditions are not supported following the removal of the group cut-off scheme.

---

## 8. User supplied tables

User supplied tables for short-range nonbonded interactions are not supported.

---

## 9. Switched interactions with PME

Switched short-range nonbonded interactions with PME are not supported.

---

## 10. Membrane embedding

Membrane embedding functionality has been deactivated.

---

## 11. QMMM

QMMM (Quantum Mechanics/Molecular Mechanics) simulations are not supported.

---

## 12. Removed auxiliary tools

Several auxiliary tools including anadock, dyndom, and morph have been removed.
