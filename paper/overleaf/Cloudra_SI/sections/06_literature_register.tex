\section{Literature sample and source-linked capability register}
\label{sec:si-literature}
\subsection{Selection and coding procedure}
The literature sample contains 85 chemical AI agent systems, architectures, or workflows in the sources collected by 27 October 2026. Each included an LLM that planned calculations, used tools, modified inputs or code, or acted on feedback during an executed molecular, atomistic, or molecular coarse-grained calculation. Each separately described system or workflow counts once; journal and preprint versions and aliases are merged. The register lists the original sources, supporting passages, and coding decisions.

Systems were classified by what their examples studied: materials, crystals, and surfaces (39); finite molecules and complexes (23); liquids, soft matter, and biomolecules (12); or multiple classes (11). A periodic box containing a protein, liquid, or polymer was classified by its contents. The 85 records cover the sampled literature; Cloudra is shown separately in the manuscript figures.

Task codes mark activities demonstrated in the sources: T, structure construction/editing; E, separately demonstrated static energy, electronic structure, or material-property calculations; O, geometry optimization/relaxation; F, vibrations/frequencies and associated thermochemistry; R, explicit reaction paths or transition-state searches, including solid-state NEB; D, molecular or quantum dynamics. A record can have multiple codes. Their counts are 61, 61, 53, 22, 11, and 37, respectively. A blank leaves the activity unencoded and the system's capability in that category open. The register identifies coarse-grained normal modes and MD-derived free-energy profiles as such.

\subsection{Source register and counting variants}
Table~\ref{tab:si-literature} links each system to its primary article or official project record and any additional sources. The files \path{companion_data/literature/systems.json} and \path{companion_data/literature/source_index.csv} give the source location and supporting description for each code. They also list the demonstrated targets, boundary conditions, aliases, system families, and access limitations. Screening and search records include a crosswalk to the 49-system review used to find sources.

The main manuscript uses separately described systems/workflows. Grouping the specified El Agente, MDAgent, MatAgent/ChatMat, and DynaMate/DynaMate2 families gives 75 records (37 materials/surfaces, 17 molecules/complexes, 11 soft/biomolecular, and 10 multiple-class records). Excluding the three official-demonstration-only systems gives 82 records (38, 21, 12, 11); additionally excluding MLP Copilot gives 81 (37, 21, 12, 11). These counting variants are retained with their definitions in the source data.
