\subsection{NI1: two requested steps and their conformer references}
\label{sec:si-ni1-chemical-objects}

The Guided NI1 study delivered methylamine addition to formaldehyde and dehydration of the resulting carbinolamine. The retained run is \texttt{batch\_94084d85d0634ca0}, under \path{campaign_data/cloudra-p2c-20261025/guided/r1/sandboxes/NI1/} in the full P2 archive. Table~\ref{tab:si-ni1-energies} lists values extracted from that run's native Gaussian outputs. All calculations use B3LYP-D3(BJ)/def2-SVP in the gas phase, charge 0, multiplicity 1. Energies are electronic energies without thermal or zero-point corrections.

The addition barrier is 28.11819 kcal/mol. For dehydration, referencing the lowest carbinolamine gives 57.92308 kcal/mol, while referencing the actual optimized IRC-side conformer gives 57.21423 kcal/mol. $I'$ lies 0.70885 kcal/mol above $I$. The lowest product and the corresponding IRC endpoint differ by 0.00006 kcal/mol. The interconversion between $I$ and $I'$ was not calculated.

For addition, the TS1 frequency geometry is the starting geometry of \path{ts/ts_0001/jobs/declaration_0006/} IRC calculations. The two actual terminal frames are the inputs of \path{ts/repair/e1_fix/rc1_side_optfreq.log} and \path{ts/repair/e1_fix/int1_side_optfreq.log}. For dehydration, the TS2 frequency and IRC calculations share \path{ts/ts_0004/jobs/declaration_0003/}; its terminal frames are the inputs of \path{s2_fix/src_optfreq.log} and \path{s2_fix/tgt_optfreq.log}. Both IRC calculations followed 24 points per direction and stopped at the point limit. All four endpoint optimizations terminated normally at the declared species with zero imaginary frequencies.

The original study also explored an extra explicit-water-assisted dehydration branch, whose reactant and product compositions include an additional water molecule. Its barrier is 36.42945 kcal/mol from its lowest reactant complex and 32.54199 kcal/mol from its local IRC-side conformer. The main-text profile displays the two requested steps with the original molecular composition.

The directory \path{companion_data/revision_20261101/ni1_chemical_objects/} supplies the geometries, energy table, figure script, and archive-relative native-output locations and hashes.
