\section{CloudraBench task catalogue}
\label{sec:task-catalogue}

CloudraBench comprises 20 Easy, 10 Normal, 10 Hard, and 10 Impossible tasks. Tables~\ref{tab:catalogue-easy}--\ref{tab:catalogue-impossible} list each task's chemistry, charge, multiplicity, conditions, and research question. The machine-readable catalogue contains task names and molecular inputs; the full task definitions also contain atom mappings, prompts, reference graphs, and scoring settings.

Method set A permits xTB, PM7, and B3LYP-D3(BJ)/def2-SVP for Easy, Normal, and Hard tasks. Method set B permits xTB and PM7 for Impossible tasks. Each answer is evaluated under its task's permitted methods. Mechanism tasks request the investigated species and elementary steps as a graph, electronic activation barriers for computed steps, and the corresponding stationary-point and pathway calculations. Stereochemical tasks also require assigning identities from the computed geometries. The tables list each task's specific question in addition to these shared requirements.

For Impossible tasks, a supported conclusion that the target is physically inaccessible under the allowed methods is a valid result.
