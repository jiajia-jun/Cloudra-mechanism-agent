# Choose a force field for the MD study

For a reproduction, recover the paper's force-field release, water model, ion parameters, ligand charges and any overrides from its inputs or supporting information. For a new study, use benchmarks that tested similar chemistry and the intended observable. Record the proposed combination and why its evidence applies. Ask the user to confirm the model when the study leaves it open. Cloudra's 50-task Benchmark scores reaction mechanisms and barriers. The comparisons here come from external MD studies. [Cloudra Benchmark](../../../benchmark/README.md)

## Protein in water

- **Folded protein.** Amber ff19SB with OPC is a candidate. On GB3, ubiquitin and lysozyme, ff19SB/OPC and the tested ff14SB setups had similar deviations from experimental backbone NMR order parameters. The ff19SB authors recommend OPC based on their comparisons. CHARMM36m with CHARMM-modified TIP3P is another documented combination. Use the exact water variant and the matching ion definitions. [ff19SB study](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.9b00591) · [CHARMM36m study](https://doi.org/10.1038/nmeth.4067) · [Knowledge card `kb:dyn03:28`](../../../../vector_store/cards.jsonl#L14921)
- **Disordered protein or flexible region.** Compare candidates such as ff19SB/OPC, CHARMM36m with its specified modified TIP3P, and a99SB-disp with its co-parameterized water. In an independent comparison, all three described huntingtin Nt19 helicity more closely than ff14SB/TIP3P, but produced different intermolecular behavior for ubiquitin and amyloid peptides. In an α-synuclein study, the estimated chain size for a99SB-disp changed with the trajectory length. Check chain dimensions against SAXS or FRET and local structure against NMR where available. Choose separate evidence for aggregation or association. [Independent comparison](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.csbj.2021.04.050) · [a99SB-disp development](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1800690115) · [α-synuclein benchmark](https://doi.org/10.1002/prot.26409)

For an approved ff19SB/OPC run, call `pdb_inspect` and `pdb_clean` as shown in [biomolecular-preparation.md](biomolecular-preparation.md). From `md/`, use `bash` to check `gmx --version` and select the installed force field. GROMACS 2026 bundles this port and the OPC-specific ion definitions. Verify the exact release on the execution node and inspect `topol.top` for the force-field, water and ion includes before solvation. [GROMACS force fields](https://manual.gromacs.org/2026.4/user-guide/force-fields.html) · [Port validation study](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.6c01592)

```bash
gmx pdb2gmx -f assembly.cleaned.pdb -o protein.gro -p topol.top -ff amber19sb -water opc
```

For an IDP chain-size check, use the whole-molecule trajectory prepared in the main skill and select the experimental construct. For example, with a single-chain `Protein` group, run `gmx gyrate -s md.tpr -f whole.xtc -sel 'group "Protein"' -o rg.xvg` to monitor mass-weighted size across runs. For a SAXS benchmark, calculate scattering from the sampled conformations and compare it with the measured scattering. [GROMACS `gyrate`](https://manual.gromacs.org/current/onlinehelp/gmx-gyrate.html) · [α-synuclein benchmark](https://doi.org/10.1002/prot.26409)

## Protein with a small-molecule ligand

In a relative binding free-energy benchmark covering 598 ligands and 22 targets, workflows with OpenFF Parsley and Sage, GAFF2.1x, and CGenFF/MATCH* gave pooled RMSEs of 1.7–1.8 kcal/mol. The OPLS3e/FEP+ workflow had a lower pooled error and used a different engine and sampling protocol. The Sage workflow's RMSE was 0.6 for galectin and 2.9 for PDE10. Use the closest target and ligand chemistry for a comparison. For affinity, compare relative binding free energies with measurements for the same ligand series. For a binding pose, compare ligand geometry and contacts with the available structure. [Hahn et al. RBFE benchmark](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.4c00417)

Start from the mapped ligand with explicit bond orders, stereochemistry and all hydrogens for the intended protonation state. If the approved Amber workflow uses GAFF2 and AM1-BCC, run these commands through `bash` where AmberTools is installed. Set `LIGAND_CHARGE` to the ligand's integer charge in the same shell call, and use the MOL2 with the intended pose.

```bash
antechamber -i ligand-posed.mol2 -fi mol2 -o ligand.mol2 -fo mol2 -at gaff2 -c bcc -nc "$LIGAND_CHARGE" -rn LIG
parmchk2 -i ligand.mol2 -f mol2 -o ligand.frcmod -s 2
```

Read `ligand.mol2` and `ligand.frcmod`. Check that the charges sum to the intended charge, the atom mapping preserves the pose and the inferred terms are suitable. In LEaP, use `source leaprc.gaff2`, `loadamberparams ligand.frcmod`, `LIG = loadmol2 ligand.mol2` and `check LIG` before building the complete system. For a CHARMM workflow, obtain compatible CGenFF parameters and inspect the penalties and corresponding terms. For OpenFF, record the actual `.offxml` release and charge method. Metal coordination and covalent ligands need an explicit model for the site and links. [AmberTools workshop](https://yorkgrouptutorials-db4433.gitlab.io/WorkshopTutorials/2026_Amber_Workshop_SSB/Day3/HandsOn6-Param.html#parameterizing-with-am1-bcc-charges) · [CGenFF parameter assignment](https://doi.org/10.1021/ci3003649) · [Knowledge card `kb:266`](https://www.umsyar.com/266) · [计算化学公社 topology discussion](https://bbs.keinsci.com/thread-428-1-1.html)

## Water–ethanol and other mixed liquids

For water–ethanol, OPLS-AA ethanol with TIP4P/2005 water is a documented all-atom candidate. Alcohol/water comparisons evaluated density, self-diffusion and Fick diffusion, and model performance depended on composition. A separate near-ambient study with TraPPE ethanol found similar density trends with TIP4P/2005 and SPC/E but different water self-diffusion performance. Choose evidence at the intended composition, temperature and pressure, and use a measurement of the property the study needs. [OPLS-AA/TIP4P/2005 diffraction study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8279560/) · [Alcohol/water transport comparison](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.molliq.2025.127870) · [TraPPE comparison](https://doi.org/10.5488/CMP.27.23201)

1. Obtain the exact component `.itp` files and the mixture's cross-interaction rules from the selected protocol. For the existing water–ethanol box, map the packed blocks to each `[ atoms ]` list and preserve counts, cell and molecule order. For TIP4P/2005, insert the fourth water site using the selected topology's `[ virtual_sites3 ]` definition. The example with 100 waters and 100 all-atom ethanols then has 1,300 coordinate sites. A united-atom ethanol model needs a template or mapping with its own site layout. [GROMACS virtual sites](https://manual.gromacs.org/current/reference-manual/functions/interaction-methods.html#virtual-interaction-sites) · [Worked water–ethanol setup, `kb:dyn07`](../../../../vector_store/cards.jsonl#L16679)
2. Assemble `system.gro` and `topol.top`. Run the main skill's `gmx grompp -pp expanded.top` command with those files. Read `expanded.top` and check `[ defaults ]` for the combination rule and 1–4 scaling, `[ nonbond_params ]` for any explicit cross terms, and `[ molecules ]` and `[ atoms ]` against the coordinates. Report the actual parameter sources and GROMACS diagnostics. [GROMACS topology format](https://manual.gromacs.org/current/reference-manual/topologies/topology-file-formats.html) · [Knowledge card `kb:dyn04`](../../../../vector_store/cards.jsonl#L15169)
3. For density, use `md_energy` as shown in the main skill with `Density` as a term, and compare the equilibrated mean with data at the study conditions. `box.json` records the starting density. For molecular self-diffusion, use `md_msd` with `molecular_com: true` and an index group selecting the chosen species. Use a trajectory sampled often enough to track periodic displacements. Fit a linear lag-time interval and account for box-size effects when comparing with experimental self-diffusion. Use a mutual-diffusion protocol if the target is Fick diffusion. [GROMACS `msd`](https://manual.gromacs.org/current/onlinehelp/gmx-msd.html) · [Diffusion and box size](https://doi.org/10.1021/jp0477147)
